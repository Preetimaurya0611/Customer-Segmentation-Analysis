# 📊 Customers Sales & Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customers Sales & Behavior Analysis is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze customer demographics, sales performance, purchasing behavior, and customer segments.

The project transforms the **Customer Segmentation Marketing** dataset into meaningful visual insights to understand customer patterns and business performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the overall customer base
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Compare revenue across different regions
- Analyze revenue by product category
- Compare revenue across customer types
- Analyze revenue by gender
- Understand customer distribution across different age groups
- Identify key patterns in customer sales and behavior

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Excel / CSV Dataset

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Customer Segmentation Marketing

The dataset contains customer-related information used to analyze demographics, customer types, sales, revenue, regions, product categories, gender, and purchasing behavior.

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides the following key performance indicators:

- **Total Customers:** 372
- **Total Revenue:** 8,458,379
- **Total Orders:** 4,620
- **Average Satisfaction:** 3.846

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

- Customers by Age Group
- Customer Type Analysis
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Customer Type
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue & Customer Insights

## 💡 Key Insights

- Repeat customers contribute the highest share of revenue among customer types.
- Electronics generates the highest revenue among the product categories.
- The South region contributes the highest revenue.
- Female customers generate slightly higher revenue than male customers.
- Customer distribution varies across different age groups.
- Customer type and purchasing behavior have a significant impact on overall revenue.


## 🚀 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to transform customer and sales data into interactive visualizations and actionable business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **customer behavior, sales performance, revenue distribution, and customer segmentation**.
